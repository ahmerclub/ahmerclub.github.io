Sector Nine: Lockdown

ArcadeFree to playNo downloadBrowser game

Fight through a fallen research facility in Sector Nine: Lockdown. Restore power, uncover secret rooms, and survive three sectors of sci-fi action.

Sector Nine: Lockdown runs on the page itself: nothing is installed, no account is created, and your progress is held by your own browser. On a phone, rotate to landscape and use fullscreen for the largest play area.

How to play

  • Mouse click or tap to play

Controls and setup

SettingRecommendation
InputMouse or touch; keyboard where the game asks for it
ScreenLandscape, then press fullscreen inside the game
ConnectionLoads once, then runs from memory — a stable connection matters more than a fast one
PerformanceClose other tabs; browser games share the same graphics chip

If it does not feel right

Stutter is almost always the device or the connection, not the game. Lower the quality setting inside the game first, close other tabs, and check that battery saver is off on a phone. The lag checklist walks through the four causes in order.
